2. Question & Answer (Pertanyaan dan Jawaban)

|       Question       |         Answer         |
| :------------------: | :--------------------: |
| What is your name ?  |    My name is Dicko    |
| Where are you from ? |   I am from Magetan    |
| Where do you live ?  | I am live in Bati-bati |
|   What do you do ?   |    I am a employee     |
|  What do you like ?  | I am like to learn IT  |

|          Question          | Answer                 |
| :------------------------: | ---------------------- |
|   What is Her/His name ?   | Her/His name is ....   |
|   Where is she/he from ?   | She/He is from .....   |
| Where does she/he `live` ? | She/He `lives` in .... |
|   What does she/he do ?    | She/He is a ....       |
| What does she/he `like` ?  | She/He `likes` to .... |
```ad-note
He/she/it :
do -> does
like -> likes
etc.
```

3. Pronoun (Kata ganti)
	- I `am` from Indonesia
	- He `is` from France
	- She `is` from Germany
	- It `is` from China
	- You `are` from Japan
	- We `are` Dicko and Nur
	- They `are` Nelly and Gabour

```ad-note
kalimat diatas menggunakan TO BE (am/are/is) yang digunakan jika tidak ada kata kerja didepannya
```

- I `like` to learn english

```ad-note
Kalimat diatas menggunakan kata kerja jadi tidak menggunakan to be
```

```ad-note
Kata sifat (Adjective) :
- I am good (Baik secara umum)
- He is kind (Baik hati)
- She is nice (Baik/ramah)
- You are busy
- We are happy
- They are sad
- It is bad
```

3. Number

```ad-note
0 = Zero (Berhubungan dengan math)
0 = Oh (Pengucapan sehari-hari)
1 = One
2 = Two
3 = Three
4 = Four
5 = Five
6 = Six
7 = Seven
8 = Eight
9 = Nine
10 = Ten
11 = Eleven
12 = Twelve
13 = Thirteen
14 = Fourteen
15 = Fifteen
```

```ad-note
Meminta nomor dengan bahasa yang normal :
- What's your phone number ?
- Can I get your number ?
- Can I have your number ?
```

```ad-note
Meminta nomor dengan bahasa yang lebih formal/polite :
- May I have your phone number ?
- Could I have your phone number ?
- Would you mind give me your phone number ?
```

4. Kata Bantu (Auxialary)

```ad-note
Kata Bantu wajib digunakan (What, Where, When, Which, Who):
Where `do` you go ? (Tidak boleh hanya Where you go)
What `do` you read ?
```

5. Daily activity

```ad-note
- In `the` morning (the dibaca de)
- In `the` `a`fternoon (Karena konsonan the dibaca di)
- In `the` `e`vening
- At night
```

